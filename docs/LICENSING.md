AHVM Community License 1.0 is a source-available license, not an OSI-approved
open-source license. See the repository LICENSE for the operative terms.

- Community permission covers individuals and organizations whose total group
  ARR is at most USD 1,000,000, including commercial use.
- Above USD 1,000,000 ARR, a separate written, paid software license is required.
  Contact sales@ahvm.app before relying on the community permission to use it.
- ARR covers all recurring products/services of the company and affiliates,
  not just AHVM-related revenue. It is not profit, funding or total annual
  revenue. The license defines monthly assessment and currency conversion.
- Hosting, support and AI-provider fees are separate. Buying a team pilot does
  not itself grant an above-threshold commercial software license.

## Earlier releases and third-party software

Earlier Apache-2.0 releases retain their granted permissions. Users may keep
using or forking those releases under Apache-2.0, including commercially above
the new threshold. The new license does not retroactively remove that option.
Deleting Git history, changing a tag or removing a download would not reliably
revoke grants made in already distributed copies.

Upstream Bhatti material, libkrucible/libkrun, Rust dependencies, Linux, Ubuntu
and bundled AI tools retain their respective licenses. LICENSE does not impose
the ARR condition on rights independently obtained for those materials. The
original Apache text is preserved in licenses/Apache-2.0.txt; see NOTICE.

## Release preparation

Include LICENSE, NOTICE and licenses/ in source and binary distributions.
Retain third-party notices and identify the actual source revisions of reused
binaries. Do not describe an existing Apache binary as having lost its earlier
permissions merely because it is redistributed in a newer bundle.

This custom license should receive qualified legal review before relying on
it for enforcement or signing customer agreements. Future contributor rights
and any commercial relicensing permission must be addressed explicitly;
changing this file does not obtain consent from another copyright holder.
